# Cross-app navigation: Teams and Topics (Lens)

A prototype, on the `concept/cross-app-navigation` branch only. It never merges, which is why it names apps and uses real file names. It draws Miky's direction of 28 October to **feel** it before anything is decided. The top level is **Teams** (how the company is organised) and **Topics** (what it is focused on now), and Folders go away. Nothing here is decided, and no decision record is Accepted for it.

## How to read it

- **The app switcher** is top left. Peek, Ship and Leaf keep the same topic open, and each shows its own part of it: Peek the conversation, Ship the projects, Leaf the documents.
- **The same sidebar in every app** has the app's home (Desk, My issues, Home), **Topics** (the ones with an end) and **Teams**. Under a team, Peek lists its spaces, Ship its projects and Leaf its documents.
- **Peek's topic** has **All** (every conversation, the topic's and its files', each headed by its file) and **Main** (the topic's own conversation and the threads someone sent up to it by hand) beside its title. The files it lists are on the right, each with the team it lives in; clicking one shows only its conversation, as a third tab beside All and Main.
- **View as** is in the person menu, top right. As Katerina, outside Engineering and Marketing, you see their front doors (*Ask Engineering*, *Bugs*, *Talk to Marketing*), but not their chats, and Engineering's projects show as "you can't open".

## The model drawn

- A file lives in **one team**, and that team decides who reads it. A topic **lists** files from any team, and listing one grants nobody anything.
- A topic is **led by a team**, Company by default. Company is everyone: its chat is *General* (#general), and @company works anywhere.
- A topic without an end is a **space**, shown under its team (one thing with one name for now). A private team has its own chat plus a public **front door** by default, and *Bugs* is a specialised front door. Something shared up from the front door goes to the team chat.
- **A space two teams share** (*Engineering × Design*) shows under both teams and is read by both.

## What it is for

Miky decides the setup after clicking through this (28 October). The question behind it comes next, as its own brief: is the team that leads a space the same as who can read it? A front door and a shared space already say no.

## Two variants to compare

**Lens** draws one sidebar, the same in every app. **Per app (sketch)** draws Miky's sketch of 28 October: the same model and the same pages, but each app finds its way around in its own shape. Click both in all three apps.

- **Peek**: a rail of **Desk, Topics, Teams and People**, with a list column beside it. Under Teams, each team's spaces sit flat under its name: *#general*, *marketing* (Marketing's own chat) and *Talk to Marketing* (its front door).
- **Ship**: **Desk**, then **Topics** (Linear's Initiatives: each with the projects it lists), **Projects** and **Issues**, then **Teams**.
- **Leaf**: Home, Open work and Starred, then a **Teams | Topics** toggle heading the tree it switches. A topic without a team of its own is led by Company.
- **One Desk**: *Needs your attention*, *In progress, you are running it* and *Activity*, the same in all three apps, with rows from every app. Opening a Leaf row from Ship's Desk goes to Leaf. Leaf calls it Home, as in the sketch.

Not drawn: Issues, Open work, Starred, a conversation with a person.

**Chosen** (Miky, 28 October, after clicking both): **Per app**, and **one Desk** in every app. Lens stays here to compare against.

**Peek's conversation, 29 October**: each message is Peek's own message card (peek-app's `ConversationCard`: reactions, the replies row, a resolution), drawn from the same shared parts, since this Storybook cannot load an app. Sending works in the shared comment box, and a file a message was sent with shows as its card under it. **The file a message was written on is now the card's heading**, not a small "on …" label beside the time. In a file's own view the heading is left out.

**Bubbling up as a mini-timeline** (Miky, same day): a thread's card reads top to bottom as heading, the first message, its replies marked with a highlight (Peek's Question, Conclusion and the rest), the replies row, then the resolution. A line down the left joins the message to each highlight and ends at the resolution. A dotted stretch of the line says how many plain replies came between two steps ("7 replies"), and the replies row moves to the end of the card, as the way into the whole thread. The resolution is Peek's green banner everywhere, laid out like a highlight: who resolved it and "Resolved" on one line, the note under it. Peek has no Decision highlight, so the decision here is a Conclusion. *Peek, Ship and Leaf are fast* draws two: *Apps read only what they show*, a short one, and *Ship opens instantly*, whose question, conclusion and resolution are paragraphs.

**The topic's header, cleaned up** (Miky, same day): the title, a lock before it when the topic is private, and the team it belongs to as the team's avatar and name (both teams for a shared space). "Led by", "Everyone" and the "N projects in Ship" link are gone; an icon at the right shows and hides the listed files.

**Clicking a message opens its thread** in a panel on the right, in place of the listed files: the message, every reply (a highlighted one with its pill), the resolution and a box to reply. ✕ closes it.

**Header and thread, second pass** (Miky, same day): All and Main sit beside the topic's title, and the Files menu and the line explaining each view are gone, since the listed files on the right open a file's conversation. The team's avatar and name moved to the right of the header, beside the sidebar toggle, in Ship and Leaf too. The thread panel's header says only "Thread"; the file it was written on heads the thread and scrolls away with it. "Led by" is gone from the listed files.

**All | Main says what it does** (Miky, same day): a segmented control with an icon and a count on each side and a tooltip saying what each holds. **Main is only what was said on the topic itself and what was sent up** from its files; a mention alone does not bring a file's message there.

**A thread reaches Main only by hand** (Miky, same day; brief *What bubbles up*, option A). Resolving a thread or highlighting a reply sends nothing. Open a thread and choose **Share** in its header. **Share is opinionated** (Miky, same day), with two choices: **Share to Main** of the topic you are on, "an important update", the default for a file's thread; or **Forward somewhere else**, to one conversation you read (a topic, a team chat, a shared space), found by word starts. A message on the topic itself is already there, so it can only be forwarded. If you like, add a line of your own. The whole thread goes, as it grows. That writes a message on the topic by you: your name, *sent this up*, your line, and under it the thread's mini-timeline. It shows in Main and in All, in the thread's place. The thread says where it was sent and by whom.

**Share** is the action's word (Miky, same day: "probably makes the most sense"); the thread says "shared this to Main in …" or "forwarded this to …".

**Update, a highlight anyone can mark** (Miky, same day): news about a project or a task, like Linear's project updates. Its colour is a placeholder until Katerina picks one. **Completing a project in Ship asks for one**: set a project's Status to Completed and a dialog asks what it delivered. Write it, and it goes to Main in each topic that lists the project as your message, with the Update pill and "Project marked Completed". Choose Not now, and only the status changes. The agent does the same from the CLI. *Widgets* opens with build 1's real resolution of 27 October as Miky's Update. To try it, complete *Widgets 2: build 2* in Ship, then open *Widgets* in Peek. Your own Update shared with no line of its own shows as the message itself, not as a shared card. Activity lists an Update someone else shared. On the topic the card has **no verb**: the sender, their line and the thread, to judge whether the content says enough. Whether and how Main marks a shared thread is still open.

- *Peek, Ship and Leaf are fast*: Miky sent *Apps read only what they show* up with a line. *Ship opens instantly* is resolved and was **never sent**, so All shows it and Main does not.
- *Widgets*: the agent sent the resolved card question up. *Navigation*: Miky sent an open question up, before it has an answer.
- **Activity, yours alone** (Miky, same day): above the listed files, newest first, the topic's mentions of you, highlights others marked, and reactions to your messages (a reaction has no time of its own here, so it shows the message's). Each row opens its thread. It is divided by day as a conversation is (Today, Yesterday, the weekday), and each row is headed by its time (Miky, same day). It never changes what Main shows anyone. As Katerina it holds only what she can open.
- **Files, as the package's `LinkCard`** (Miky, same day; was the row widget), with no app label, since the card says what it is: a project's card shows its summary, or its summary and issues, never the latest conversation (Miky, same day); a document's its summary; each says how much was said here; a click shows the file's conversation in the topic. Activity shows about three rows and scrolls inside, and has no "Only you see this" line.
- **The sender's line is read by everyone who reads the topic**, also people who can't open the file, and the form says so. **Someone who can't open the file** sees the sender and their line, and a locked line in place of the thread: view as Katerina, who is not in Engineering, on any of the three.
