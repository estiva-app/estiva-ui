# Text comments: a word, a passage, whole blocks

A prototype, on the `concept/text-comments` branch only (project *Leaf 1: write a page together and comment on it*, issue *Comment on a word, a sentence or several blocks, and see it highlighted in the text*). It grows Katerina's `proto/text-comments` (estiva-ui) and her Leaf page (leaf `proto/leaf-app`, option A, the comments column) by what was decided since: a comment can be on a passage across blocks or on whole blocks, and words that changed are not the same as blocks that were deleted.

Nothing here is wired to a relay, and none of it ships from this branch. **Katerina approves or redraws it**; Leaf's build matches the approved stories.

## How to read it

Every story is the same page, live, opened in a different state. Use the `leaf` theme in the toolbar.

- **On a few words** — the words are highlighted; the open one is underlined.
- **On a passage across paragraphs** — from the middle of one paragraph to the middle of the next: one highlight across both.
- **On two whole paragraphs** — both paragraphs are filled, edge to edge; the card says "On 2 paragraphs".
- **Words that changed** — Ana commented on the heading "Aims", and it now says "Goals". Nothing is highlighted; the card keeps its place beside the heading and says *Ana Duarte quoted: "Aims" · No longer in the text*.
- **Blocks deleted** — the paragraph a comment was on is gone. Its card moves to *On deleted blocks*, with the words as quoted.
- **Commenting on a passage / on a whole paragraph** — select across blocks and press Comment in the strip, or use a block's handle and its Comment row. What you selected stays marked while you write; the line over the box says what the comment will be on.

Try it: type over highlighted words (say, change "owns" in "owns the press notes"). The highlight goes, and the card turns into the quoted line at once.

## Decided (Miky)

1. A comment can be on a word, a sentence or several blocks: one anchor, a run of blocks plus the words where the selection starts and ends (13 Oct).
2. Inside one block it remembers its words; across blocks the words where it starts and where it ends; whole blocks no words, and it grows with them (15 Oct, record *a-comment-remembers-where-its-selection-starts-and-ends*).
3. Blocks there, words not: the comment stays beside its block with no highlight, never listed with deleted ones (15 Oct; the look is Katerina's).
4. The words are shown as what the commenter quoted, *"Ana Duarte quoted: … · No longer in the text"*, never as the page's own words: the relay does not check a quote (15 Oct). Peek says the same.

## For Katerina: the look, each drawn one way here

1. **Whole blocks** are filled edge to edge, so they read differently from words. Or: fill only their text, like words; or a bar in the margin.
2. **Overlapping comments** stack their fills; a click opens the narrower one.
3. **The column is in the order of the text** (yours was the order they were written), so a card whose words changed stays beside its block. The whole-document comments come last.
4. **A card's first line**: words in quotes; a passage as *"first words … last words"*; whole blocks as *On the paragraph*, *On 2 paragraphs*, *On 3 blocks*; changed words in italic, muted.
5. **Deleted blocks** get their own section, closed, like Resolved.
6. **A reworded card, opened**, shows nothing in the text and scrolls its block into view.
7. Option B (the bubble on the words) is not redrawn: your advice was A.
