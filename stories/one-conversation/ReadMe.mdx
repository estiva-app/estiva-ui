# One conversation: the same comments, replies and Resolve in every app

A prototype, on the `concept/one-conversation` branch only (project *One conversation in every app*, issue *Prototype: one conversation in Peek's column, Ship's Activity and Leaf's column*). It draws the one conversation every app will place (records *a-conversation-is-one-shared-thing-and-only-its-colours-are-the-apps* and *anyone-who-may-comment-resolves-or-reopens-a-thread-and-the-newest-counts*, both Accepted): the comment and reply cards, the thread, Edit and Delete, Resolve with a note, the deleted line, "edited" with its time, a link alone on its line, quoted words, the person chip, reactions and the words for what went wrong.

**Miky judges it first, then Katerina.** The build issues match the stories she approves. Nothing here is wired to a relay, and none of it ships from this branch.

## How to read it

**Places** draws the same conversation where each app puts it, each in its own theme:

- **Peek topic**: the comments in the main pane, with the thread in the 380 px column beside it.
- **Peek, between two people**: the same, with Resolve too (Miky, 28 October).
- **Ship Activity**: up to 768 px, with the thread opening under its comment.
- **Leaf column**: 384 px beside the page, with the thread opening under its comment.
- **Phone**: 375 px.

**States** draws every state at 384 px. Pick `signal`, `ship` or `leaf` in the toolbar to see each one in each app's colours.

Everything is live. You can react, open a thread, reply, resolve with a note, reopen, and edit or delete your own comments (Person one is you). In a reply box, `/` → **Reply and resolve** resolves the thread as the reply is sent. **Write "refuse" in a send or an edit** to see it refused.

## What it settles, and what stays open

Settled (Miky, 20 and 28 October): one conversation, with only the colours each app's own. Resolve is in every app, and anyone who may comment resolves; the newest resolve counts. A resolved thread folds in place. Replies open in Peek's column, and under the comment in Ship and Leaf. No Highlights.

For Katerina:

1. **Menu words**: "Edit"/"Delete" (drawn), or "Edit message"/"Delete message".
2. **Urgent bar**: grey (drawn), or red. Compare *States → Urgent, grey bar* and *Urgent, red bar*.
3. **Refusal words**: plain sentences without "relay". The box says "Not sent: try again in a moment. Your text is back in the box."; a reaction says "Your reaction wasn’t added. Try again in a moment."; a file says "Too large to send."
4. **The resolved line and the folded thread**: their look, where Reopen sits, and the note field (*States → Resolving*).
5. **The quoted words**: the wording "Reworded since" and "No longer in the text" (*States → Quoted words*).
6. **The box's height on a phone with the keyboard open**, and reactions on a Leaf card.
