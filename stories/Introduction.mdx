# @estiva-app/ui

The shared foundation of every Estiva app — Peek, Ship, and whatever comes
next: the **design tokens** (colour, type, radius, shadow) and a **small
set of primitives** built on them.

**Tokens are the contract. Components are a convenience.** An app must not
diverge from the token names. It may fork any component, freely, and owes
nothing back — a fork is information about the seam, not a failure; say so
on the package's ticket, and never bend your view around a component.

Use the **Theme** toolbar anywhere in this Storybook: the same names render
under each app's values — `signal` is what Peek renders, `ship` what Ship
renders.

## What lives here, and what does not

- Here: anything an app with documents and no conversations could use
  unchanged. Its stories need no app data.
- Not here: anything that knows what a topic, an issue, a huddle or a
  folder is. That belongs to the app, in the app's own Storybook.

## Where it comes from

The token names and the `signal` values are Peek's, verbatim — Peek is the
visual reference and must render unchanged after consuming this package.
`ship` is Ship's palette, ruled by its designer. Primitives are extracted
from production one at a time, screenshot-compared before and after — and
shared never means averaged: each surface keeps its own measurements unless
a unification is explicitly ruled.
