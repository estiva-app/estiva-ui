# @estiva-app/ui

The shared foundation of every Estiva app's interface: the **design tokens**
(colours, type, radius, shadows) and, built on them, a **small set of
primitives** — avatars, chips, buttons, inputs.

**Tokens are the contract. Components are a convenience.** An app must not
diverge from the token names; it may fork any component, freely, and owes
nothing back.

Start with **Design Tokens**. Use the **Theme** toolbar to see the same names
under each app's values: `signal` is what Peek renders, `ship` what Ship
renders.

## What lives here, and what does not

- Here: anything that could be used unchanged by an app that has documents
  and no conversations. Its stories need no app data.
- Not here: anything that knows what a topic, an issue, a huddle or a folder
  is. That is the app's, and stays in the app's own Storybook.

## Where it comes from

The names and the `signal` values are Peek's, verbatim; Peek is the visual
reference and must render unchanged after consuming this package. `ship` is
Ship's palette, ruled by its designer. The primitives are extracted
from Peek one at a time, each screenshot-compared before and after.
