# @estiva-app/ui

The shared foundation of every Estiva app's interface: the **design tokens**
(colour, type, radius, shadow) and a **small set of primitives** built on
them.

**Tokens are the contract. Components are a convenience.** An app must not
diverge from the token names. It may fork any component, freely, and owes
nothing back — a fork is information about the seam, not a failure; say so
on the package's ticket, and never bend your view around a component.

## Where things are

The sidebar is sorted by what a part is for, in the order a screen is built:
the frame, the page, then what goes in it.

| Heading | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Start here** | These pages: the package, how to start, how to choose, the tokens. |
| **App frame** | The chrome around every screen: the shell, the top bar, the sidebar, the rail. |
| **Page structure** | How a page is cut up: columns, panels, headers, sections, tabs, cards, lines. |
| **Buttons & links** | What you click to do something or go somewhere. |
| **Forms & fields** | Typing or choosing a value, and the form that sends it. |
| **Menus & windows** | Anything that floats over the page: a tooltip, a menu, a dialog. |
| **Status & feedback** | What is going on: a state, a message, loading, empty, an error. |
| **People** | Faces, names, and who is in something. |
| **Writing** | Writing text: the boxes, what opens while you type, blocks, links in a document, pasting, and reading it back. |
| **Links & references** | How a link to another thing is drawn: in a sentence, on its own line, as a card, on hover. |
| **Conversation** | Comments, replies and reactions. |

Use the **Theme** toolbar anywhere in this Storybook to see every page
under each of the package's themes: the same token names, a different look.

## What belongs here

- In the package: components that know nothing about any product — built
  from tokens, generic over their content, with stories that need no app
  data.
- In your app: anything that knows your product's objects. Build it there,
  out of these parts, and keep its stories in your app's own Storybook.
