# CardUnder

The one card added under a piece of text, and the ✕ that removes it.

<Canvas of={CardUnderStories.WhileWriting} />

## When

- **while writing** — under the box: the ✕ always shows.
- **after sending** — `revealOnHover`: the ✕ shows when the pointer is over the card or the ✕ has focus, and always on a screen that cannot hover, so a tap reaches it.
- **no ✕** — no `onRemove`: to anyone but who wrote it.

<Canvas of={CardUnderStories.AfterSending} />

## When not

- A link alone on its line, with no card chosen → **LinkRow**.
- A part of something longer: it has no ✕ → **PartCard**.

## How

```tsx
import { CardUnder } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<CardUnder link={link} onRemove={isAuthor ? removeCard : undefined} revealOnHover={sent} />
```

- **Which link gets the card is the app's**, by the rule every app reads by (`defaultCardOf` in `@estiva-app/protocol`). This draws the one it is given.
- `onRemove` is the app's: before sending it drops the card from what will be sent; after, it sends the edit that removes it.
- It is at most 480px wide.

## What it owns

| It does | So you never write |
|---|---|
| Places the ✕ at the card's top corner | a remove button of your own |
| Reveals the ✕ on hover and focus, and always shows it on touch | a hover or touch check |

## Keys

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| Tab | Reaches the ✕, and shows it when it was waiting for hover |
| Enter, Space | Removes the card |
