# PageHeader

The bar at the top of a page: what the page is, and what acts on all of it, each in its own place.

<Canvas of={PageHeaderStories.Everything} />

It is ContainerHeader's bar — 48px, a hairline under it, 16px in at each
end — with a place for each thing a page's bar holds. At the left, 8px
apart: an icon or a face, a lock, the title, something right after it. At the
right, 12px apart: quiet counts, when it last changed, the members, then the
page's buttons, 4px apart.

## When

- **The top of any page**: a page of one thing, opened from a list, with its
  name and the buttons that act on the whole of it.
- `leading`: what kind of page it is, or its state, as a 16px icon; a face
  for a page that is one person's.

<Canvas of={PageHeaderStories.WithAFace} />

- `private`: a lock before the title, when only its members can open it.
- `after`: something that belongs to the title, right after it — a small
  `Button` that opens it elsewhere, a `Chip`.

<Canvas of={PageHeaderStories.AfterTheTitle} />

- `meta`: quiet counts at the right, a dot between them; `done: true`
  for words about what is finished.

<Canvas of={PageHeaderStories.Counts} />

- `time`: when it last changed, in words, 12px italic; the exact time is in
  a tooltip.

<Canvas of={PageHeaderStories.EditedTime} />

- `members`: who is in it — a **MembersPill**.
- `actions`: the page's own buttons — a **Toolbar** of `ToolbarButton`s,
  `surface={false}`. A button that shows and hides the right column is
  one of them, its icon filled while the column is open.

<Canvas of={PageHeaderStories.ColumnToggle} />

- A **Breadcrumb** in the title's place, for a page reached down a trail.

<Canvas of={PageHeaderStories.Breadcrumb} />

## When not

- The bar of a side column — details, a thread → **Panel**, which draws its
  own bar from `title` and `actions`.
- The bar of a list beside the page → **ListColumn**.
- The bar across the whole frame, with the logo and the search field →
  **TopBar**.
- A heading for one part of the page → **SectionHeader**.

## How

```tsx
import { MembersPill, PageHeader, Toolbar, ToolbarButton } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<PageHeader
  leading={<IconSquareRounded size={16} stroke={1.5} />}
  title="Item one"
  meta={['6 open', { label: '2 done', done: true }]}
  time={{ label: 'Edited 2 minutes ago', exact: '31 October 2026, 14:02' }}
  members={<MembersPill members={members} onClick={openMembers} />}
  actions={
    <Toolbar aria-label="Page actions" surface={false}>
      <ToolbarButton tooltip="More" aria-label="More">
        <IconDotsVertical size={16} stroke={1.5} />
      </ToolbarButton>
    </Toolbar>
  }
/>
```

- **It is the bar, and nothing under it.** The page under it is yours: a
  `ScrollArea` that takes the rest of the column (`min-h-0 flex-1`), or a
  **Panel** that takes it in `header`, in place of its own bar.
- A string `title` is one line and is cut short with an ellipsis when the
  room runs out; everything at the right keeps its size. A node — a
  `Breadcrumb`, an `InlineText` — is drawn as given, and cuts itself short.

<Canvas of={PageHeaderStories.LongTitle} />

- `leading` is 16px. A bare icon takes the secondary text colour; a face is
  an `Avatar` at `size={16}`.
- **The tooltips are under the pointer only.** The lock is named "Private"
  to assistive technology as well; the exact time is not, and Tab does not
  stop on the words. That is a known gap.
- The quiet things keep 12px clear of the buttons by themselves: write no
  padding between them.

## What it owns

It does these by itself. Write none of them around it.

| It does | So you never write | Refused when written by hand |
|---|---|---|
| Draws ContainerHeader's bar: 48px, the hairline under it, 16px in at each end | a header row of your own | ContainerHeader used on its own in an app |
| Keeps the counts, the time and the members 12px apart, and 12px clear of the buttons | `gap-3`, `pr-2` of your own around them |  |
| Cuts a long string title short and keeps everything else whole | `truncate`, `min-w-0`, `shrink-0` |  |
| Draws the counts in `MetaText`, a dot between them | a dot or a count style of your own |  |
| Draws the time 12px italic, with the exact time in a tooltip | `text-caption italic` and a `WithTooltip` of your own |  |
| Names the lock "Private", in a tooltip and to assistive technology | an `aria-label` and a tooltip on an icon |  |

## Keys

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tab | Moves through the bar in reading order: what is after the title, the members, then the buttons |
| Arrow keys | Move along the `Toolbar` of buttons, which is one Tab stop — the Toolbar's own keys |
