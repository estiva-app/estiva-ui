# ListSection

A list under its heading on a page, with its count.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When

- **Each list on a page** that holds several: the heading names what is under
  it, the count says how many, nothing on the heading to press. The list sits
  4px under it; stack the sections 24px apart in the page's column.
- **While the list is read**: leave `count` out, and put the skeleton in the
  list's place. A count not known yet is not 0.

<Canvas of={Stories.BeingRead} />

- **Nothing in it**: the empty line in the list's place.

<Canvas of={Stories.Empty} />

## When not

- A heading with actions on it, or one that folds → **SectionHeader**, or
  **CollapsibleSection**.
- A list in its own column, with a bar over it → **ListColumn**.
- A panel's title over a body that scrolls → **Panel**.

## How

```tsx
import { ItemList, ListSection, SkeletonList } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<div className="flex flex-col gap-6">
  <ListSection title={heading} {...(rows ? { count: rows.length } : {})}>
    {rows ? <ItemList aria-label={heading} rows={rows} /> : <SkeletonList rows={3} />}
  </ListSection>
</div>
```

- `data-*` marks go on the section, for the app's own tests.

## What it owns

| It does | So you never write | Refused when written by hand |
|---|---|---|
| The heading, its count, no fill under the pointer | `SectionHeader hover="none"` |  |
| 4px between the heading and its list | a `section` with `flex flex-col gap-1` |  |
