# PartCard

A part of something longer, shared live: its blocks as they are now, under the title of what it is part of.

<Canvas of={PartCardStories.Default} />

## When

- **ready** — what it is part of, as a card starts: its kind in a file's 36px tile, its title as the link, and "A part of ABC-2" under it; then the blocks the app draws as `children`, behind a quote bar that starts where the tile does. The apps draw the blocks with **RichText** `size="small"`.
- **long** — with `maxHeight`, a long part is cut behind Show more, under the quote bar. Its title is the way to it, in the app it lives in: there is no "Open in …" button (Katerina, 31 Oct).
- **loading** — at a card's height.
- **unavailable** — one row: "A part of this issue not available", in the app's word for it, else its kind's. Not the reader's to see.
- **deleted** — what it is part of was deleted, as a trusted deletion naming it says (SPEC §13.6.1): the same row, "A part of this issue deleted".
- **detached** — what it is part of is still there and the part's block is gone: the same row, "This part was deleted".
- **unaddressable** — what it is part of has no blocks to take a part of: the same row, "This part can't be shown", its words opening the whole of it (`href`). Nothing was deleted.

The five are SPEC §13.6.1's, with its words; a reader tells them apart.

<Canvas of={PartCardStories.Detached} />
<Canvas of={PartCardStories.Unaddressable} />

## When not

- A link to the whole thing → **LinkCard** or **AttachedCard**.

## How

```tsx
import { PartCard } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<PartCard state="ready" kind={kind} heading={title} partKey={key} href={hrefAtThePart}>
  {blocksDrawnByTheApp}
</PartCard>
```

- The blocks are the app's to draw, with its own renderer.
- There is no ✕: to send without the part, the writer deletes the link.

## What it owns

| It does | So you never write |
|---|---|
| Marks the blocks as quoted from elsewhere | a border of your own |
