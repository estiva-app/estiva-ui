# Resolution

Who resolved a thread and the note they left: a green chip on the comment's top edge, and a line inside the thread.

<Canvas of={Stories.OnAComment} />

## When

- **ResolutionNote** on a resolved comment: the kit's green **Chip**, "Resolved by Person two", then the note in
  white inside it (Katerina's pick, 31 October). **ConversationCard** draws it on its top edge from `resolved`: write
  nothing on the card yourself.
- **ResolutionHistory** inside a **Thread**, among the replies, in time order: every resolve and reopen.

<Canvas of={Stories.Note} />
<Canvas of={Stories.LongNote} />
<Canvas of={Stories.History} />

## When not

- A status on its own → **Chip**.

## How

```tsx
import { ResolutionHistory, ResolutionNote } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<>
  <ResolutionNote by="You" note="Done." />
  <ResolutionHistory events={[{ by: 'You', note: 'Done.', at: '12:10 PM' }]} />
</>
```

- The same in every app, in each app's colours.
- The note is cut at the end of the line, inside the chip; who, when and the whole note are on hover. In the thread,
  the note is whole.
- `look` keeps the earlier ways, for the proposal boards (Prototype/Resolved options) until they go. Leave it out.

## What it owns

Nothing. It only draws. Clicks and keys are yours.
