# QuotedLine

Above a comment on a part of the page: the words it is on, set off by a bar in the document's highlight colour, under the face below.

<Canvas of={Stories.Words} />

## When

- On a **ConversationCard** about a part of the page, in its `about`.

<Canvas of={Stories.Reworded} />

<Canvas of={Stories.Gone} />

## When not

- A whole block quoted in the words → **PartCard**.

## How

```tsx
import { ConversationCard, QuotedLine } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<ConversationCard author={author} time={time} about={<QuotedLine words={quote} state="reworded" />}>
  {words}
</ConversationCard>
```

- Two lines at most; the whole words are in the tooltip.
- A part with no words, a picture or a block: `label` and its kind's `icon`.
- Made for the card's `about`: the bar lines up with the 24px face under it.

## What it owns

Nothing. It only draws. Clicks and keys are yours.
