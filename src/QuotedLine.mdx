# QuotedLine

On a comment on a part of the page: the words it is on, in the page's own highlight, under the name and above the comment's words.

<Canvas of={Stories.Words} />

## When

- On a **ConversationCard** about a part of the page, in its `about`.
- Above a **CommentBox** whose new comment will be on that part, in its `about`: the same line the posted card will carry.

<Canvas of={Stories.Reworded} />

<Canvas of={Stories.Gone} />

## When not

- A whole block quoted in the words → **PartCard**.

## How

```tsx
import { ConversationCard, QuotedLine } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<ConversationCard author={author} time={time} about={<QuotedLine words={quote} state="reworded" />}>
  {words}
</ConversationCard>
```

- Two lines at most; the whole words are in the tooltip.
- A part with no words, a picture or a block: `label`, its name in your words, on the grey: the yellow is only for words still in the page.
- Made for the card's `about`: the card puts it under the name, where the words start.
- Read away from the page it quotes (another app, a list of comments from several pages): `href`, where the words are in that page, and the words open it there; `external` when it is another app's. Beside the page itself, none.

<Canvas of={Stories.Linked} />
