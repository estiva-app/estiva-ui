# ConversationPlace

Above a conversation shown away from where it was said: how it is related, and where it was said.

<Canvas of={Stories.MentionedIn} />

## When

- A list of conversations from elsewhere on a page's Activity: each card under the line saying how it is related to the page (`relation`, "Mentioned in") and the place's name (`place`).
- Another relation, in your words: the same line, the same look.

<Canvas of={Stories.OnAnItemUnderIt} />

- The place's name has not come yet: leave `place` out. The relation is drawn alone, never a placeholder: "Loading…" beside a real comment reads worse than a line that has not said where it is from yet.

<Canvas of={Stories.PlaceNotNamedYet} />

<Canvas of={Stories.LongPlace} />

## When not

- The conversation is on this page: its card alone → **ConversationCard**.
- A comment on a part of the page: what it is on goes inside the card, in its `about` → **QuotedLine**.

## How

```tsx
import { ConversationCard, ConversationPlace } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<ConversationPlace relation="Mentioned in" place={nameOf(address)} data-relation="mention">
  <ConversationCard author={author} time={time} readOnly onClick={openWhereSaid}>
    {words}
  </ConversationCard>
</ConversationPlace>
```

- The words are yours: `relation` is how the conversation is related, `place` the name your app gives the place.
- The card is yours too: it goes in as `children`, under the line, 4px below it.
- The place's name is cut to the column.
- Anything else you pass (a `data-…` mark) lands on the row, so you can find it.

## What it owns

Nothing. It only draws. Clicks and keys are yours.
