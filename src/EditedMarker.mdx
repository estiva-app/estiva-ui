# EditedMarker

"(edited)" beside a time, with when it was edited on hover. Nothing is drawn when it was never edited.

<Canvas of={Stories.Edited} />

## When

- Beside the time of anything people wrote and can edit: the cards draw it from `edited`.

## When not

- A time on its own → **MetaText**.

## How

```tsx
import { EditedMarker } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<EditedMarker edited={editedWords} />
```

- The words in `edited` are the app's: its own way of saying a time.

## What it owns

Nothing. It only draws. Clicks and keys are yours.
