# PinnedComment

The comment a thread answers, pinned at the top of the replies column: who, when, and its first line.

<Canvas of={Stories.Pinned} />

## When

- At the top of a **Thread** in the right column. Thread draws it from its `start`.

<Canvas of={Stories.Deleted} />

## When not

- The comment itself, in the list → **ConversationCard**.

## How

```tsx
import { Thread } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<Thread start={{ author: author, time: time, words: words }} onClose={onClose} box={box}>
  {replies}
</Thread>
```

- Give `time` its day: no day line stands above it here.
- `at`, its moment: the time says the exact date and time under the pointer, as a comment's does. In a **Thread**, `start.at`.

## What it owns

| It does | So you never write | Refused when written by hand |
|---|---|---|
| Says the exact date and time under the pointer, from `at` | a tooltip round the time |  |

Clicks and keys are yours.
