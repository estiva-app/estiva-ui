# UrgentMark

Urgent and new: a small warning mark on its tint, in the unread dot's 24px slot.

<Canvas of={Stories.Urgent} />

## When

- In place of **UnreadDot** on something urgent and not read yet: a comment, a reply, a row of the navigation.

## When not

- New and not urgent → **UnreadDot**.
- A count of urgent things → **Chip**.

## How

```tsx
import { UnreadDot, UrgentMark } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<>{isNew && (urgent ? <UrgentMark /> : <UnreadDot />)}</>
```

- It says nothing to a screen reader: say "urgent" in the row's own words.

## What it owns

Nothing. It only draws. Clicks and keys are yours.
