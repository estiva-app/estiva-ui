# ListTable

A list of things on a page, under column headings: each row leads to its thing, a few short facts in columns, and an action at its end.

<Canvas of={ListTableStories.Default} />

## When

- **A page that lists things to find and open**: every topic, every team's
  places, every project — each row its name and what it is about, then its
  facts (its team, who is in it, when it was last active), then what you can
  do from the list (Join).
- `columns`: the facts after the name, each with its heading and its width —
  `small` 80px (a number, a few faces), `medium` 96px (a date), `large`
  112px (a name).
- `keep`: which columns give way when the list runs short of room — `low`
  ones first, then `high` ones. The name and the end never do. The list
  measures **its own** width, not the window's: beside a sidebar it gives way
  as it would in a small window.

<Canvas of={ListTableStories.Narrow} />

<Canvas of={ListTableStories.Narrowest} />

- `end`: the rows hold their own control at the end, and the list keeps 64px
  for it in every row, so the columns stay lined up.
- A cell is words, or a part: an **AvatarGroup** with its number in
  **MetaText**, 6px apart. A cell not read yet shows a dash.

<Canvas of={ListTableStories.NotReadYet} />

- It takes the whole width it is given. The name is at most 384px and its
  facts sit beside it; the room left goes before the end, which keeps the
  list's right edge. A name stretched across the page sat far from its
  facts.

<Canvas of={ListTableStories.OnAWidePage} />

## When not

- The rows of a sidebar → **NavItem**, in a **Sidebar** or a **NavTree**.
- The list beside the thing that is open → **ListColumn**.
- A link alone on its line, in a file's text → **LinkRow**.
- A table someone writes, in a document → the editor's table.

## How

```tsx
import { Button, ListTable, ListTableRow } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<ListTable
  aria-label="Topics"
  columns={[
    { label: 'Team', width: 'large' },
    { label: 'Members', width: 'small', keep: 'low' },
    { label: 'Active', width: 'medium', keep: 'low' },
  ]}
  end
>
  {topics.map((topic) => (
    <ListTableRow
      key={topic.id}
      href={topic.href}
      label={topic.title}
      description={topic.summary}
      icon={<KindIcon kind="topic" />}
      cells={[topic.team, topic.members, topic.active]}
      end={topic.yours ? undefined : <Button size="small" variant="outlined" onClick={() => join(topic)}>Join</Button>}
    />
  ))}
</ListTable>
```

- **The row is the link**: a click anywhere on it opens its thing, and the
  keyboard ring goes round the whole row. What is in `end` stays its own
  control, above the link.
- It draws the headings, the row's look (8px corners, the fill under the
  pointer, no edge), the widths, the dashes and the giving way. Write none of
  them; give it words and parts.
- Its headings line up with their cells because both come from `columns`:
  never draw a heading row of your own over it.

## What it owns

| It does | So you never write |
|---|---|
| Draws the column headings over the cells | a row of headings by hand |
| Hides columns by its own width, `low` first | `hidden sm:block` on a column |
| Draws a row's fill, its 8px corners and no edge | `rounded-md hover:bg-bg-hover` on a row |
| Makes the whole row the link, its end above it | a covering link and `relative` by hand |
| Keeps the name at most 384px, the facts beside it, the end at the right | a width of your own on the name |
