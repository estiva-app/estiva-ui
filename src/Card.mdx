# Card

A box that stands for one thing — drawn as its frame: 8px corners, a fill and a
hairline. What goes inside, and the space around it, is yours.

<Canvas of={CardStories.AllFills} />

## When

- **It draws no padding and no layout**: both are yours.

A box is a card when it stands for **one thing you could name**, and there are
many of it or you treat the whole box as that thing — open it, point at it,
move it.

- **`surface`** — on the page. The default hairline.
- **`elevated`** — on something already filled, like a column of cards. The
  default hairline.
- **`inset`** — inside something filled, like a message. The subtle hairline.
- **`none`** — a hairline alone, the subtle one.
- `href` — the whole card is a link; its hairline goes one step stronger on
  hover.
- `hover="fill"` — a card in a feed: it lights up when pointed at, so the light
  says which one you are on. `quietUntilHover` hides its hairline until then.
- `onPress` — the card opens what it stands for: a press anywhere on it but on
  a button, a link or a field inside it, and Enter or Space once Tab reaches it.
- `hovered` — hold that look while the pointer is elsewhere: a card whose own
  menu or picker is open stays lit, because the menu opens outside the card.
- `selected` — the one you are on. `active` — the one being changed in place.
  Neither answers the pointer, and `hovered` changes neither.
- `attention` — a hairline that asks to be looked at: `accent` for something
  new, `warning` for something urgent.
- `unreadable` — it stands for something that could not be read: the hairline
  is dashed.

## When not

- You type in it → **TextInput**, **Textarea**, or your editor's box — a field,
  not a card.
- It frames an area holding many different things → a panel, or
  **DialogShell**.
- It is small and sits in a line → **Chip**, **InlineChip**, or **Button**.
- It stands in while the card loads → **Skeleton**, shaped like the card.
- It tells the reader something rather than being a thing → **Banner**.

## How

```tsx
import { Card } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<Card href={itemUrl} onClick={navigate} className="flex flex-col gap-2 p-3">
  <span className="text-body-2-strong text-text-primary">Item one</span>
  <span className="text-caption text-text-secondary">A line about the item.</span>
</Card>
```

- It draws no padding and no layout of its own: put them in `className`, or let
  the content pad itself.
- With `href` it renders `Link`'s anchor, with every anchor prop passed through,
  so it changes page as **Link** does and the address stays real.
- A card that opens on a press takes `onPress`, not `onClick`: it works out for you
  whether a press was on the card or on a control inside it, a menu it opened
  (outside it in the page), a double click or words being picked, and it is
  a Tab stop that Enter and Space open. `onClick` stays for a card that only
  needs the click.
- Without `href`, a card that responds to a click gets `cursor-pointer` from its
  `hover` and your `onClick` — the selected one too, since it can open again;
  the one being changed does not. Controls inside it should stop their own
  clicks from reaching the card.
- Keep a card lit while its menu is open by passing `hovered` from your own
  state: set it when the pointer enters, and clear it when the pointer leaves
  and nothing of the card's is open.

- `clip` cuts what is inside to the card's rounded corners — a strip or a
  picture that runs edge to edge. Without it their square corners show.
- **Placed from outside, never restyled.** `className` takes space, size,
  flex and grid, and position; a colour, a text size, a border, a corner or a
  shadow is refused by the lint (`estiva/no-restyled-part`). What is inside the card is yours: its padding and its words go on
  your own element inside.

- **Planned:** the inside layout of a linked object's card, in migration stage 7.

## What it owns

It does these by itself. Write none of them around it.

| It does | So you never write | Refused when written by hand |
|---|---|---|
| With `href`, becomes a link: Link's anchor, with a real address | an anchor around the card |  |
| Without `href`, shows it can be clicked when you pass `onClick` or `onPress` | `cursor-pointer` |  |
| With `onPress`: a press on the card, never on a button, link, field or menu inside it, nor a double click or picked words; a Tab stop, opened by Enter and Space | a click guard (`closest('button')`), a key handler, a `tabIndex` |  |

## Keys

With `onPress`:

| Input | What happens |
|---|---|
| Tab | stops on the card |
| Enter / Space | on the card: opens it; on a button, a link or a field inside it: that control's own |
