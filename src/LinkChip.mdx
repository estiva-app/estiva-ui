# LinkChip

A link in a sentence: its icon, its key, its title cut short, and its status as a shape. Up to 40 characters, never wider than its line.

<Canvas of={LinkChipStories.InASentence} />

## When

- **ready** — a link the app has looked up. Hovering it shows its card (**LinkPreview**); a tap or a click opens it.
- **loading** — still being looked up: the icon, which the address already says, and a bar.
- **unavailable** — not the reader's to see: "Issue not available", in the app's word for it (its type's, else its app's name).
- **deleted** — `deleted` on the state, when a trusted deletion names it (SPEC §13.6.1): "Issue deleted". A reader tells the two apart; the words never say which a hidden file is.
- **written as a URL** — `url` on the state: a link written as an app URL that cannot be shown stays that URL, a plain link, deleted or not available alike (SPEC §13.1). The words above are only for a `nostr:` reference.
- `preview={false}` — no card on hover, where a card would cover what the reader is choosing from.

## When not

- A link alone on its line → **LinkRow**.
- The one card attached to a piece of text → **AttachedCard**.
- A person, a place or a label with no lookup behind it → **InlineChip**.

## How

```tsx
import { LinkChip, type LinkState } from '@estiva-app/ui'

// The app looks the link up and hands in where that stands.
const link: LinkState = found ? { state: 'ready', linked: found } : gone ? { state: 'unavailable', kind: 'issue', noun: 'Item' } : { state: 'loading', kind: 'issue' }
<LinkChip link={link} />
```

- **The app looks it up; the chip draws.** `linked` (`Linked`, from `@estiva-app/ui`) carries the words, the `href`, the status and the owner as the app's lookup found them. The chip fetches nothing.
- `linked.href` opens it. An address in this app goes through `AppProvider`'s `navigate`, so the page does not reload.
- Words a person wrote (`kind: 'message'` with an `author`) lead with their face and read "Name: words".

## What it owns

| It does | So you never write |
|---|---|
| Shows the card on hover, and none on a screen that cannot hover | a hover card of your own, a touch check |
| Cuts a long title short | a truncation of your own |
| Says "not available" in words, quieter than a link | a missing-reference chip of your own |
