# LinkRow

A link alone on its line: its kind in a file's tile, the title and its key under it, and on the title's line its state, comments and faces.

<Canvas of={LinkRowStories.EachKind} />

## When

- **ready** — its kind in the 36px tile a file's type has (**AttachmentCard**), the title as the link, and under it the key and what it is ("ABC-2 · Issue"), in the app's word for it, else its kind's. On the title's line: its status or its ring, its comment count, then the owner, members or authors at the far right. Hovering it shows its card; a tap opens it.
- **ready** and archived — `linked.archived`: "Archived" in the line under the title ("ABC-2 · Issue · Archived"). `linkStateFrom` sets it, and words for its values, from an archived file.
- **words a person wrote** — on one line, as a quote is cut to one: their face in the tile, their name, the words cut to fit, the time.
- **width** — at most 480px, in a box being written in too.
- **unread** — the count is brighter, with its dot.
- **loading** — the row in bars, in the row's own shape.
- **unavailable** — a row's shape, "Issue not available", with where it may still open (`openIn`): a small `Button` "Open" with an open-out icon, a link to a new tab, the app in its name and tooltip ("Open in Ship"), as Peek's file header has it. It is the one way there: the words are not a link. This is also what a card draws when it is not available: **LinkUnavailable**, from this file.
- **deleted** — `deleted` on the state, when a trusted deletion names it (SPEC §13.6.1): the same row, "Issue deleted", and no way out.
- **written as a URL** — `url` on the state: the URL as it was written, a plain link in the row's place, deleted or not available alike (SPEC §13.1).

## When not

- A link in a sentence → **LinkChip**.
- The one card attached to a piece of text → **AttachedCard**.

## How

```tsx
import { LinkRow } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<LinkRow link={{ state: 'ready', linked }} />
```

- The whole row opens `linked.href`; the conversation count opens `linked.conversation.href` on its own. With no address (an empty `href`, as for another app's file with none over https), its title, or its count, is words and not a link.
- `linked.conversation.unread` is the app's to say, and only for a member of it.

## What it owns

| It does | So you never write |
|---|---|
| Covers the row with its link, with the count above it | a click handler on the row |
| Shows the card on hover, and none on a screen that cannot hover | a hover card of your own |
| Holds the row's height while it loads and when it is not available | a placeholder height of your own |
