# Thread

A comment's replies, with the box to write one: in the right column under its own header, or under its comment.

<Canvas of={Stories.Column} />

## When

- `place="beside"`: in the right column, beside the comments (two columns). The comment is pinned at the top, then "Replies", then the replies; the box stays at the bottom.
- `place="under"`: under its comment, in one column. The replies hang from a line under the comment's face, with no fill until pointed at, and the box is under them. **ConversationLayout** makes the comment and its thread one card.
- `resolved`: "Resolved" at the top of the column, in green.
- `notice` in place of the box: why no reply can be written.

<Canvas of={Stories.Resolved} />

- A link to a reply shows it: `showInConversation`, scrolled to the middle and flashed.

<Canvas of={Stories.Shown} />

- `seen`: its replies are watched, and a new one keeps its look until it has been seen.

<Canvas of={Stories.Seen} />

<Canvas of={Stories.Under} />

## When not

- The comments and where their threads open → **ConversationLayout**.
- Any other column → **Panel**.

## How

```tsx
import { ReplyCard, Thread } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<Thread start={start} resolved={resolved} onClose={close} box={replyBox}>
  <ReplyCard author={author} time={time}>
    {words}
  </ReplyCard>
</Thread>
```

- A reply is read by being seen in its thread: `useSeenOnScreen(key)` for the thread, handed to `seen`, holds the ids of the replies that have been on screen a moment. Draw each reply's `isNew` from it.
- Beside, where it scrolls is the thread's: it opens at its newest reply, goes to each new **ReplyCard** drawn in it (a
  line of your own among them takes it nowhere), and stays at the bottom while cards and pictures fill in. A reply
  deleted moves nothing. Key each reply by its own id: another thread's replies are then drawn afresh, and it opens at
  their newest, with or without a `start`.
- Its replies are yours to put in time order. Who resolved it is said on the comment, not among them.
- Still reading: `loading`. Nobody replied yet: nothing in `children`.
- An app whose empty thread said nothing keeps its silence with `empty={null}`; `closeLabel` names Close in its words for a screen reader.
- `start.id` is put on the pinned comment as `data-thread-start`, and each **ReplyCard**'s `id` on it as `data-reply-id`, as ConversationLayout's comments carry `data-conversation-item`. Give every reply its `id`.
- A link to a reply or to the comment shows it: `showInConversation(id)` scrolls it to the middle of the column and flashes it, and answers `false` when it is not drawn; `showInConversationWhenDrawn(id)` waits for a thread just asked to open, up to 3 seconds, and stops when a newer link is pressed. Never a wrapper of your own round a reply to carry its id, and never a flash of your own.

## What it owns

| It does | So you never write | Refused when written by hand |
|---|---|---|
| Pins the comment at the top, and keeps the box under the replies | a header and a fixed footer |  |
| Beside: opens at its newest reply, goes to each new one, holds still while cards load | a scroll to the bottom, a ResizeObserver |  |
| Placeholders while reading, and a line when nobody replied | a loading and an empty state |  |
| "Resolved" in green at the top of the column | a chip |  |
| Says which replies have been on screen a moment, with `seen` | an IntersectionObserver and a timer per reply |  |
| The id on each reply and on the pinned comment, and the flash when a link shows one | a wrapper with an id, a flash in your CSS |  |
