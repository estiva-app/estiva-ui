# PreviewCard

More of a thing, on hover.

A list shows one line per thing because a list has to. This is where the rest
of that thing goes: a card that opens beside the row when the pointer rests on
it, holding whatever the caller draws — named values, a longer description, a
picture, a few related lines. It closes when the pointer leaves, and you can
move into it, which is what makes it different from a tooltip.

<Canvas of={PreviewCardStories.Default} />

## When

- **A row is a summary and the thing has more to it** — the case this exists
  for. The row stays one line; the card carries the rest.
- **A name or a reference that could say what it refers to** without a trip
  to another page.
- **Anything worth reading before deciding to open it.** If the reader would
  otherwise click, look, and come back, this saves the round trip.

The test is whether the content is *worth pointing at*. A label is a
**WithTooltip**. Something you might read, scroll or copy is this.

<Canvas of={PreviewCardStories.Loading} />

## When not

- A word for an icon-only control → **WithTooltip**. The real difference is
  not length: a tooltip is `pointer-events: none` and cannot be reached,
  and this can. If the thing you are showing has anything in it worth
  pointing at, it is a preview card.
- A list of actions → **Menu**.
- Content that needs a click to appear, or that has controls in it →
  **Popover**.
- Something the person must read to proceed → put it in the page.

## How

```tsx
import { PreviewCard } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<PreviewCard content={<ConversationPreview id={row.id} />} wrapperClassName="block w-full">
  <ListRow …/>
</PreviewCard>
```

- `content` renders **only while the card is open**, so a preview that
  fetches does not fetch for every row on screen.
- It opens **350ms** after the pointer comes to rest — Peek's own number — and closes **200ms**
  after it leaves — long enough not to flash a card at every row while
  crossing a list, and long enough to cross the gap into the card.
- It prefers the **right** of the trigger and flips when that side has no
  room, staying 12px from the trigger and 8px clear of every screen edge.
  Pass `side` to prefer another.
- Width, padding and max height are yours through `className`; the default
  is 360px wide and capped at 300px or the room available, whichever is
  smaller. A preview of a conversation is not the size of a preview of a
  person.
- The trigger wrapper is `inline-flex`; a full-width row wants
  `wrapperClassName="block w-full"`.

## Keys

A preview card is a pointer affordance and has no keyboard trigger: nothing
in it may be the only route to anything. Escape closes an open one.

| Input | What happens |
|---|---|
| pointer rests on the trigger | opens after 350ms |
| pointer moves into the card | it stays up |
| pointer leaves both | closes after 200ms |
| Escape | closes |
