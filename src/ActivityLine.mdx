# ActivityLine

One thing that happened, in a list of them: a mark for what kind of thing it is, who did it and what, when; under it where it was said and the words.

<Canvas of={ActivityLineStories.Default} />

## When

- A list of what happened, in a card, a popover or a page's dot: replies to
  you, mentions, reactions to your messages.
- `mark`: what kind of thing it is, a 16px icon beside the first line — the
  face and the name — never centred on both lines.
- `did`: what they did, after their name, in the app's words.
- `where`: the place it was said, before the words, on a list that mixes
  places; absent on the place's own list.
- `seen`: an Earlier line — the name in the secondary text.

<Canvas of={ActivityLineStories.Seen} />

- `href`: it opens where it was said, as a link (Cmd- or Ctrl-click opens a
  new tab). Absent, the line is a button and `onClick` acts.
- New and Earlier: each a **MenuSection** of lines, so the headings and the
  lines start on one edge.

<Canvas of={ActivityLineStories.AList} />

## When not

- An action in a menu → **MenuItem**.
- The message itself, with its replies and reactions → **ConversationCard**.
- A row in a sidebar that leads to a place → **NavItem**.

## How

```tsx
import { ActivityLine } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<ActivityLine
  person={{ name: person.name, picture: person.picture }}
  did="replied to you"
  time="2h"
  mark={<IconArrowBackUp size={16} stroke={1.5} />}
  where="Group one › Item one"
  words={message.preview}
  href={message.href}
/>
```

- It is a menu row's look: a fill under the pointer and no edge, 8px
  corners, 36px at the least. Write no card, edge or padding around a line.
- Its words have their sizes: the name `body-2-strong`, what they did
  `body-2`, the time in a count's type, the second line `caption`. Write no
  type of your own.
- `data-*` marks pass to the row, for the app's tests.

## What it owns

Nothing. It only draws. Clicks and keys are yours.
