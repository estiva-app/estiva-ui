# ExploreList

Every place you can open, to find one and join it: tabs with their counts, a search, and Join on a live one you are not in.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When

- **A page to browse every place of one kind** and join one, as a browse
  page does. It opens on All; Yours, Others and Archived beside it, each
  with its count.
- **Search** finds a name by the starts of its words, in any order, in the
  open tab. Nothing found says so, with what was asked.

<Canvas of={Stories.NoMatch} />

- **Join** only on a live place you are not in. While it is on its way
  (`joining`), its button waits.

<Canvas of={Stories.Joining} />

- **Nothing listed**, **loading**, **failed** (with Try again) and **signed
  out** each say so in words.

<Canvas of={Stories.Failed} />

## When not

- Items of several kinds in Status, People, Progress and When → **ItemList**.
- Other columns, or no tabs → **ListTable** with **ListTableRow**.
- Tabs alone → **Tabs**.

## How

```tsx
import { ExploreList, exploreWords, KindIcon } from '@estiva-app/ui'

const words = exploreWords({ one: 'item', many: 'items', a: 'an' })

<ExploreList
  words={words}
  icon={<KindIcon kind={kind} />}
  lists={lists}              // { all, yours, others, archived }, or 'loading' | 'failed' | 'signedOut'
  tab={tab}
  onTab={setTab}
  onRetry={retry}
  onJoin={(row) => join(row.id)}
  joining={joining}          // the ids whose Join is on its way
/>
```

- `exploreWords` makes every word of the page from what it lists: the title,
  the tabs, the search, the empty lines and the failure.
- `title` replaces the bar's title where the page is one team's.
- Each row: its `team`, its `members` (`{ faces, count }`) and `active`, in
  the app's words ("2h ago"). One left out is the dash.
- It draws the page's **Panel**: give it the page's column, full height.

## What it owns

| It does | So you never write | Refused when written by hand |
|---|---|---|
| The panel with its title, 24px in and 24px between its parts | a `Panel` and its padding |  |
| The tabs and the search on one row, the search 256px | a hand-laid row of `Tabs` and `SearchInput` |  |
| Search by the starts of words | a filter of your own |  |
| Team, Members and Active, and Join at the row's end | `ListTable` columns and a `Button` per row |  |
| Empty, no match, loading, failed and signed out | `EmptyState` and `SkeletonList` per state |  |
