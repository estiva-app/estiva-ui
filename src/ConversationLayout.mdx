# ConversationLayout

Where a conversation's cards sit: two columns, or one 384px column beside the page.

<Canvas of={Stories.TwoColumns} />

## When

- `threads="beside"`, two columns: the comments fill the page; an open thread stands beside them, 384px.
- `threads="under"`, one column: the comments are the 384px column beside the page; a thread opens under its comment, and the two become one card.
- With two columns, `header` puts the comments' own **PageHeader** over their column only, and the thread runs the page's full height beside it.
- With two columns, `aside` is what the 384px column holds while no thread is open: the page's own **Panel** beside its comments (an overview). A thread opened takes its place.

<Canvas of={Stories.TwoColumnsAside} />
- With two columns, each comment's `day` names its stretch with a line that stays at the top while the stretch scrolls (Peek's day lines).

<Canvas of={Stories.TwoColumnsDays} />

<Canvas of={Stories.TwoColumnsArchived} />

- With one column, an item that is `resolved` waits closed under "Resolved" at the end, and one whose part was deleted (`gone`) under "On deleted blocks" before it, each with how many. A group opens by itself for the thread open in it. With two columns they stay in their place.

<Canvas of={Stories.OneColumn} />

<Canvas of={Stories.Apart} />

## When not

- One thread on its own → **Thread**.
- A list of places → **ListColumn**.

## How

```tsx
import { ConversationLayout } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<ConversationLayout threads="under" items={items} open={openId} onOpenChange={setOpenId} box={commentBox} />
```

- Which layout is the app's: where a conversation sits is its own.
- The right column is 384px in every app.
- The app keeps `open`; the layout says when it changes, with `onOpenChange`. Give every comment a `thread`, a
  **Thread** with its box, even one with no replies yet: pressing it opens it to its box. An app that builds only the
  open thread gives a function, `thread: () => threadOf(comment)`: it is called only while that one is open.
- Draw no column of your own for the thread or for the panel beside the comments: `aside`. The column catches what
  throws in it, and says so in its place; the comments stay.
- Write nothing on the cards for opening: no `onClick`, `selected`, `onReply`, or the replies row's `onOpen` and
  `open`. The layout gives each **ConversationCard** its place, and the card opens its thread from it. What a card is
  given still wins.
- Two columns, where the comments scroll is the layout's (Peek's way, in every app): they open at the newest; they
  follow what arrives only while the reader is at the bottom (within 80px); the reader's own comment, sent from `box`,
  brings them down to it as it is drawn, and a refusal leaves them where they are; and they hold still while cards and
  pictures fill in. Give the conversation its name, `rememberAs` (its address), and going back to it lands where it was
  left, in this tab. `useLeftAt(name)` says when the reader left it away from the newest, for your "New" line in
  `before` over the first comment written since. Keep no place of your own: `bodyRef` and `onBodyScroll` only read the box.

<Canvas of={Stories.TwoColumnsLong} />

- Each comment's `day` draws the app's day lines,
  each staying at the top while its own day scrolls under it; `before` is a line just above one comment (a "New"
  line); `below` is under the comments (a **ReactionHorizon**); `top` stands at the top however few the comments are
  (an **ArchivedNotice**). Few comments sit on the box, as a chat does. `empty` may be the app's own **EmptyState**,
  or `null` for nothing.
- A comment is read by being seen: `useSeenOnScreen(key)` for the conversation, handed to `seen`, holds the ids of the
  comments that have been on screen a moment (half of the card, or 120px of a taller one, for 1.5 seconds, in a tab
  that is not hidden). Draw `isNew` from it; mark the conversation read from it. `key` `null` watches nothing, until you
  have judged what is new. Never an observer of your own.

<Canvas of={Stories.TwoColumnsSeen} />

- A link to a comment shows it: `showInConversation(id, { comments: true })` scrolls it to the middle and flashes it
  (the open thread's pinned comment first, when it is that one's); `false` when it is not drawn. Each comment carries
  its `id` as `data-conversation-item`. See **Thread** for replies.
- The box at the foot of a thread that opens takes the caret, at its end, without scrolling: the layout tells it with
  `caretOnOpen`, which **CommentBox** reads. Nothing to pass; a box outside a layout never takes it this way.

## What it owns

| It does | So you never write | Refused when written by hand |
|---|---|---|
| Opens the thread beside the comments, or under its comment | the column and where it goes |  |
| A press anywhere on a card, its replies row or Reply opens its thread; in one column a second press closes it | an `onClick` on each card |  |
| Marks the open comment: beside, its card selected; under, its replies row turned open | `selected`, the row's `open` |  |
| Opens and closes in place: the comment is never redrawn and does not move, open or closed | a wrapper that changes when it opens |  |
| One column: resolved comments and comments on deleted blocks closed under their names at the end, opened for the thread open in them | the groups, their counts, opening one |  |
| Puts the caret in the box of a thread that opens, without scrolling | a focus call |  |
| Two columns: opens at the newest, comes back to where it was left, follows only a reader at the bottom, goes down to the reader's own comment, holds still while cards load | a scroll policy, a place per conversation, a ResizeObserver |  |
| Placeholders while reading, and a line when there are none | a loading and an empty state |  |
| Says which comments have been on screen a moment, with `seen` | an IntersectionObserver and a timer per card |  |
| The right column at 384px, in every app: the open thread, or the page's `aside` | a width, a column, its error boundary |  |
