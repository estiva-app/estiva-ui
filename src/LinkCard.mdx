# LinkCard

A link's card: what a row cannot hold, and the changes the reader may make from it.

<Canvas of={LinkCardStories.Default} />

## When

- **ready** — it starts as its row does: its kind in a file's 36px tile, the title as the link on one line, cut short when long, and under it the key and what it is ("ABC-2 · Issue"); on the title's line, at the right, its ring, its conversation count and its faces, in that order, as a row's. Then its summary, the items inside it in a box of their own, its newest conversation as a quote, and a footer: the pickers, or who wrote it and when. No summary, the title alone.
- **width** — at most 480px, in a message, a document and a box being written in alike; in a box it sits on the files' fill, its values as words until it is sent.
- **footer** — only when there is something for it: a title and a summary alone end at the summary.
- **read only** — a picker the app passed no `onChange` for reads as words in the same place: the reader may not change it. `readOnly` makes every value words, as the hover preview does.
- **words a person wrote** — their 24px face, their name, the time beside it as the link, and the words.
- **loading** — the card it will become in bars, at its own height: `shape` from `loadingShapeOf(linked)` the last time it drew, or the kind's usual card. **LinkCardLoading**, from this file.
- **unavailable** — one row, as **LinkRow** draws it.

## When not

- A link alone on its line, where the card was not chosen → **LinkRow**.
- The one card attached to a piece of text, with its ✕ → **AttachedCard**, which draws this.
- A part of something longer → **PartCard**.

## How

```tsx
import { LinkCard } from '@estiva-app/ui'

const linked = {
  ...fromLookup,
  status: { value: statusKey, options: statuses, onChange: mayChange ? setStatus : undefined },
  owner: { value: ownerKey, options: people, onChange: mayChange ? setOwner : undefined },
  ownerLabel: 'Owner',
}
<LinkCard link={{ state: 'ready', linked }} />
```

- **Whether the reader may change it is the app's.** Pass `onChange` only to a reader who may; anyone else sees words.
- The picker offers only the options it is given: to let the reader set nobody as the owner, include an option for it.
- `linked.latest` is drawn whenever it is given: pass it only to a reader who may read it.
- `linked.conversation.unread` only for a member of it.
- **From interop's projection, build it with `linkStateFrom(object, unread, options)`** (or `linkedFrom` for a read object), so every app draws one thing as one card: its key, its 7 most recently changed items, a ring over all of them, and pickers through its own actions when `options.onAct` is passed (words once it is archived). When it last changed (`changedAt`) reads "2h ago", the same words in every app unless the app passes `whenOf`; who made it, the key its address is signed by, shows as a face when the app can name them and nothing else on the card names a person. The app passes `hrefOf(object, top)`, `previewText(body, people)` and, for a link not yet read, its kind and noun.

## What it owns

| It does | So you never write |
|---|---|
| Draws the pickers, or the same values as words | a read-only variant of your own |
| Holds its own height while it loads | a placeholder height of your own |
| Opens each item inside it on its own | a row click handler |
