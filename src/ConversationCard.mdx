# ConversationCard

One comment, the same in every app: who, when, the words, files, cards, reactions and the way into its replies, with the actions on hover and the ⋯ menu.

<Canvas of={Stories.Comment} />

## When

- **A comment** in any conversation: in the comments beside a page, or filling the page with its thread beside it (**ConversationLayout**).
- `own` on yours: Edit (with `editor`) and Delete (with `onDelete`) in its menu. The card asks before it deletes.
- `resolved`: "✓ Resolved" by the time, with who, when and the note on hover; "Person two → the note · time" above its replies row. Nothing of the comment is hidden: its words, cards, files and reactions stay.
- `noun`: what the delete question calls it, "Delete this comment?" by default; a direct message says `message`.
- `about` on a comment on a part of the page: a **QuotedLine** under the name, above the words.
- `isNew`, `urgent`: the dot or the urgent mark, and a hairline, until it is read.

## When not

- A reply in a thread → **ReplyCard**: no replies of its own, no Resolve.
- A link to something, alone → **LinkCard**; a card someone attached sits in the words as an **AttachedCard**.
- A row in a list of places → **NavItem**.

## How

```tsx
import { ConversationCard } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<ConversationCard
  author={author}
  time={timeOf(comment)}
  edited={editedWords}
  reactions={{ reactions: reactions, options: options, nameOf: nameOf, viewer: me, onToggle: (emoji: string) => react(comment, emoji) }}
  replies={{ count: replyCount, faces: faces, last: lastTime, isNew: hasNew }}
  resolved={resolution}
  onResolve={(note) => resolve(comment, note)}
  onReopen={() => reopen(comment)}
  own={isMine}
  editor={(done) => editBoxFor(comment, done)}
  onDelete={() => remove(comment)}
  onClick={() => openThread(comment)}
>
  {wordsOf(comment)}
</ConversationCard>
```

- **The words are yours to draw**: text, people named, links, attached cards and quoted blocks, with **RichText** and the link parts. The card gives them their size and colour.
- **Edit opens your box**: `editor` draws your **CommentBox** edit form; call `done` when it saves or cancels. To open it again after a refused save, hold `editing` yourself.
- **Resolve opens the dialog**: **ResolveDialog**, then `onResolve(note)`. Answer `false` when it was refused; the dialog stays.
- A press on the card is `onClick` (its thread opens); a press on a button, a link or anything marked `data-interactive` inside it is not.
- The strip shows while the card is pointed at or has focus, and holds while its reactions or its menu are open.

## What it owns

| It does | So you never write | Refused when written by hand |
|---|---|---|
| Shows its actions while pointed at, and holds them while a menu is open | hover state and an outside-click check |  |
| A card that opens on a press is a Tab stop: reaching it shows its actions, Enter or Space opens it | a `tabIndex` and a key handler |  |
| Asks before it deletes | a confirm dialog |  |
| Opens the Resolve dialog, and closes it once resolved | a dialog and its open state |  |
| Says it is resolved, with who and the note, and hides nothing of it | a resolved line or chip of your own |  |
