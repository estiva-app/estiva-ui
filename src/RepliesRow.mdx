# RepliesRow

The way into a thread, under its comment: who replied, "2 replies", the last reply's time, and how many are new ("2 new") until they are read.

<Canvas of={Stories.Several} />

## When

- Under a comment with replies: **ConversationCard** draws it from its `replies`.
- `onOpen` where the row itself opens the thread (one column, the thread under its comment); without it, the card around it does.
- `open` while that thread is open under it: the chevron turns down and the row stays marked, and a press closes it. The whole card around it opens and closes the thread too.
- `newCount`, how many replies are new: "2 new", and the row's name says it too ("3 replies, 2 new, open the thread"). Given, it decides; 0 says nothing new.
- `isNew` alone, for an app that knows only that one is new: "1 new", and the row's name stays the count.

<Canvas of={Stories.NewSeveral} />
<Canvas of={Stories.New} />
<Canvas of={Stories.Open} />

## When not

- A count on its own → **MetaText**.
- Faces on their own → **AvatarGroup**.

## How

```tsx
import { RepliesRow } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<RepliesRow count={3} faces={faces} last="12:02 PM" newCount={2} onOpen={() => openUnder(comment)} />
```

- Faces and the last time show in Signal; in the other themes the row is the icon and the count.

## What it owns

| It does | So you never write | Refused when written by hand |
|---|---|---|
| Says "1 reply" or "2 replies" | the plural |  |
| Shows how many are new until they are read ("2 new"; "1 new" for `isNew`), in the warning colour when urgent | the chip and its colour |  |
