# ConversationHeading

Which place a conversation is on, inside its card, when one list mixes conversations from several places.

<Canvas of={Stories.OnACard} />

## When

- One list mixes this place's own conversations with conversations said on other places: each from elsewhere carries its place's icon and name at the top of its card, in the card's `heading`. This place's own carry none.

<Canvas of={Stories.WithWhatItIsOn} />

- A name longer than the column is cut.

<Canvas of={Stories.LongName} />

## When not

- A conversation shown away from its place, with how it is related ("Mentioned in") above its card → **ConversationPlace**.
- A comment on a part of the place: what it is on goes in the card's `about` → **QuotedLine**. Both can be on one card: the place, then the author, then the words it is on.

## How

```tsx
import { ConversationCard, ConversationHeading, KindIcon } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<ConversationCard
  author={author}
  time={time}
  heading={place ? <ConversationHeading icon={<KindIcon kind={kindOf(place)} />} name={place.title} onOpen={() => openPlace(place)} /> : undefined}
>
  {words}
</ConversationCard>
```

- `icon` is drawn in the face's column, so the name lines up with the author's. Give it 16px; it is hidden from screen readers, the name says it.
- `onOpen`, or `href`, makes the name a button or a link. A press on it opens the place, never the thread: the card leaves a press on a button or a link alone, and the rest of the card still opens the thread.
- A deleted comment keeps its heading: its replies still belong to that place.
- In a `ConversationLayout`, pass it on every card from elsewhere; the layout puts them in your order (time order, mixed with this place's own).

## What it owns

Nothing. It only draws. Clicks and keys are yours.
