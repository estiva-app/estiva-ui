# Divider

A hairline in `border-subtle`. Horizontal it is inset 12px each side;
`orientation="vertical"` stands it up to its row's height, no inset. It is
Base UI's `Separator`, which gives it the `separator` role and its
orientation for assistive tech.

<Canvas of={DividerStories.Default} />

## When

- Between sections of a panel, a rail or a card.
- Vertical: between clusters in a toolbar or a dense row.

<Canvas of={DividerStories.Vertical} />

- With a `label`, words sit in the middle of the line — a date between two
  days of messages, or where "new since you last read this" begins.
  `tone="warning"` for the second: the label in the warning colour, the
  lines in its wash. The line is named by its label for assistive tech.

<Canvas of={DividerStories.Warning} />

- `look="section"` between stretches of a list, in the list's own voice: a
  date, where the answers to the first item begin. The words are
  `SectionLabel`'s muted voice and are cut short with an ellipsis where there
  is no room; the lines run edge to edge, 8px in, and the whole line is 24px
  tall. A node may stand for the words; it names the line by them. It is
  always muted: where what is new begins is the caption look's `warning`,
  in the same list.

<Canvas of={DividerStories.ADate} />

<Canvas of={DividerStories.New} />

- `sticky` keeps it at the top of its list while its stretch scrolls under
  it, until the next one takes its place.

<Canvas of={DividerStories.Sticky} />

## When not

- Between groups in a menu → **MenuSeparator**: this line with its own 4px
  above and below.
- Between every row of a list — spacing does that; a hairline per row is
  noise.
- To title what follows → **SectionLabel** or **SectionHeader**; a heading
  separates better than a line.

## How

```tsx
import { Divider } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<Divider />
<Divider orientation="vertical" />
<Divider label="Today" />
<Divider label="New since you last read this" tone="warning" />

<Divider look="section" label="Replies" />

// Each line first in the box that holds its stretch.
{days.map((day) => (
  <div key={day.id} className="flex flex-col">
    <Divider look="section" label={day.label} sticky />
    {day.rows}
  </div>
))}
```

- **Inside a `Menu` or a `Popover` it has no inset**: the hairline runs the
  width of the rows. The panel tells it so; nothing is passed in.

- The line is 1px; the room around it is yours. The section look brings its
  own: 8px at each side, 6px above and below; a `className` such as
  `px-4 py-2` changes it.
- **`sticky` sticks to the nearest box that scrolls**, and only while the box
  that holds it is in view: put each line first in the box that holds its
  stretch, as above. It is drawn on the surface's fill (`bg-surface`), so the
  rows pass under it unseen; a list on another fill is not covered yet.

## What it owns

It does these by itself. Write none of them around it.

| It does | So you never write | Refused when written by hand |
|---|---|---|
| Says what it is to assistive technology: a separator | `role="separator"` | ✓ |
| Keeps its 1px line in a scrolling column | `shrink-0` |  |
| Cuts a long label short with an ellipsis, in the section look | `min-w-0 truncate` around the words |  |
| Stays at the top of its list while its stretch scrolls, with `sticky` | `sticky top-0 z-10 bg-bg-surface` |  |
