# MetaText

The small figure beside a name: a time, a resolve line's time, a count. In Signal the mono micro-type with even digits.

<Canvas of={Stories.Time} />

## When

- A time beside a name.
- `tone="secondary"` and `tone="success"` for counts of open and resolved.
- `size="chip"` beside a small name.

<Canvas of={Stories.Counts} />

## When not

- Words a person reads as a sentence → plain text in the caption size.
- A status → **Chip**.

## How

```tsx
import { MetaText } from '@estiva-app/ui'

<MetaText>11:08 AM</MetaText>
```

- It never wraps.

## What it owns

Nothing. It only draws. Clicks and keys are yours.
